The technology

A piston pushed by magnetism, not only by fire

The block, the crank and the cylinder head stay familiar. What changes is the cylinder wall: copper windings are wound into the space around each bore, and the piston itself is magnetic.

1

Energise the coil

The winding generates a field that repels the magnetic piston and drives it down the bore.

2

Fire cylinders in sequence

Parallel cylinders repeat the action in order, turning linear push into rotation.

3

Share one crankshaft

Both the magnetic stroke and the combustion stroke deliver torque to the same shaft.

How it works

Two power paths, one crankshaft

The electric stroke

1

Current enters the copper winding surrounding the cylinder.

2

The field repels the magnetic piston and pushes it down the bore.

3

Parallel cylinders repeat the push in firing order.

4

The crankshaft turns — rotational motion reaches the wheels.

The combustion stroke

1

Intake — the charge is drawn into the cylinder.

2

Compression — the piston rises and squeezes the mixture.

3

Power — the plug fires and drives the piston down.

4

Exhaust — spent gas is cleared through the head.

Because both paths act on the same crankshaft, switching between them needs no second drivetrain — and no speed limit.

Engineering honesty

What stands in the way — and how we answer it

Challenge

Eddy current losses

Remedy

Re-engineer the coil winding pattern to cut induced circulating currents.

Challenge

Heat generation

Remedy

Design a dedicated coolant flow architecture around the winding cavity.

Challenge

Loss of magnetism during combustion

Remedy

Line the chamber with an insulating protective layer to shield the piston.

Challenge

Low torque output

Remedy

Lengthen the piston stroke and tune the ECU for the magnetic drive profile.

Every one of these is a design problem, not a physics wall — and each has a defined line of attack.

Application of the project

One drivetrain platform, two vehicles

The same solenoid hybrid architecture scales from a retro roadster to a full four-wheel platform.

The Bike

A retro roadster in the spirit of a Royal Enfield, built around the solenoid engine

Dual battery + fuel tank

A primary and secondary battery run alongside a conventional fuel tank for range flexibility.

Snapdragon AI as ECU

An on-board Snapdragon chip acts as the engine control unit, tuning efficiency in real time.

Regeneration on engine braking

Under engine braking the block spins freely without power — the solenoid coils turn that motion back into current.

Multi-sensor ride control

A sensor array continuously monitors the ride for stability and smoothness.

Universal charging port

One port, compatible across common charging standards.
